2b · Word Counting Exercise

Explore word frequencies with Voyant Tools

NoteBy the end of this exercise you will have
  • Uploaded the interview transcripts to Voyant Tools
  • Identified the most frequent words and what they suggest
  • Read words in context and adjusted the stop word list
  • Noted at least one pattern worth investigating further

Get the Data

Download the original interview transcripts from the Zenodo archive. You will upload the .txt files directly into Voyant.

Download from Zenodo

Step 1 — Upload the Data

Go to voyant-tools.org, click Upload, and select one or more .txt files from the Zenodo archive.

You can upload one file at a time or select all six to analyze the interviews together.

Step 2 — Review the Cirrus Word Cloud

Look at the top 5–10 most frequent words.

  • What themes do they suggest?
  • Are there words that seem surprising given the interview topic?

Step 3 — Check the Trends Panel

Select three or four words and look at how they are distributed across the six interviews.

  • Are any words used predominantly by one or two speakers?
  • Do any words appear only in one document?

Step 4 — Use Contexts for One Word

Click on a word in the cloud to open the Contexts view. Read 5–6 instances in their surrounding sentences.

  • Does the word always appear in the same type of sentence?
  • Does any speaker use it differently from the others?

Step 5 — Adjust Stop Words

In the Cirrus panel, click Define Options to edit the stop word list. Copy and paste words exactly as they appear in the cloud to add them. The list will be sorted alphabetically after you save.

Try removing conversational words like like, really, just, think, or also. How does the word cloud change?

Step 6 — Discuss

  • What did the word cloud confirm that you already expected?
  • What surprised you?
  • Which patterns would you want to investigate further with a richer method?
